# Loss evidence: what we can say about quotes and requests that go quiet

Research date 2026-09-30. Every quote below was checked against the live page unless marked otherwise. Evidence tags: **[H]** primary source or filing, **[M]** vendor or self-reported survey, **[L]** anecdote or thin evidence.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **No published source measures what share of quotes never get a yes or a no.** The closest figures are "never became a job" (ArboStar, tree) and self-reported win rates (Jobber). So the "quiet %" we drill into owners has to be their own number, from their own export. Before they upload, show "never became jobs", not "went quiet".
2. **The ServiceTitan 11–15% line is real, but it is narrower than the way it gets quoted.** It is 47% of $10M+ contractors, not "the biggest shops". The sample is mostly HVAC, plumbing, electrical and roofing.
3. **`claims.ts` has a wrong fact.** ServiceTitan 2023's "71%" is business volume from word-of-mouth referrals. It is not "71% of jobs from repeat customers". Fix it (§6).
4. **No reliable published close rate exists for fence, painting or cleaning.** A widely copied "PCA 2025: painters close 22%, 38% never decided" figure has no traceable source. Don't use it.
5. **Lead cost by trade can be cited with its source:** cleaning, painting and landscaping (LocaliQ, Google search ads), and tree, painting, landscaping and cleaning (99 Calls, Google LSA). Fence has only a thin agency figure.

## 1. Share of quotes that never get a yes or no, or never convert

**ArboStar tree CRM data [M]**
- Exact wording: "Across the aggregated data, the average estimate-to-job conversion rate sits at 61%." By size: "Small (3–9 employees) 62.3%", "Growing (10–25) 65.3%", "Large (26+) 56.7%".
- Method: "aggregated and anonymized CRM data from tree service companies using Arbostar over the full year of 2025". It is "not a before-and-after study". Sample size is not given.
- Article: Thomas Heathcote, 1 Apr 2026. https://arbostar.com/education-hub/where-tree-service-companies-actually-lose-money
- **Line:** "In tree work, about 4 in 10 estimates never turn into a job (ArboStar, a year of tree-company records, 2025). Drop your file and see how many of yours never got a yes or a no."

**Jobber 2026 Home Service Trends Report [M]**
- Exact wording: "69% of pros report a win rate of more than 50%. More than a third say they close over 70% of quotes." Also: "36% report winning more than 70% of jobs, 33% report win rates between 51% and 70%."
- Method: "Survey of 1,050 home service business owners across the United States, conducted through Conjointly in December 2025… ±3 percentage points at the 90% confidence level." https://www.getjobber.com/home-service-trends-report/
- Derived: 64% of owners win 70% or less, so for them at least 3 in 10 quotes don't become work.
- **Line:** "Only about a third of owners win more than 7 in 10 of their quotes (Jobber, 1,050 owners). For everyone else, 3 in 10 or more go nowhere."

**TreeBuzz forum, owner "Evo", 2 Jun 2020 [L]**
- Exact wording: "1/3 of my unapproved quotes are 100% non responsive, about 1/3 just couldn't find it (or shopped and chose me anyway), and 1/3 or less say they got someone else to do the job sooner."
- https://www.treebuzz.com/forum/threads/customer-interactions-with-quotes.42984/
- Use it as an owner-voice quote only, never as a statistic.

**"Quiet isn't no" (deferral)**
- Angi 2025 State of Home Spending Pulse [M]: "71% of homeowners have put off at least one home project this year." "The most commonly postponed projects include bathroom remodels (12%), interior painting (10%), and window replacements (10%)." Method: 1,000 US homeowners who hired a pro in the past year, Pollfish, Apr 15–16, 2025. https://www.angi.com/press/angis-2025-state-of-home-spending-pulse-report
- HIRI Q1 2026 tracker, via Angi [M]: "Nearly three in four homeowners (73%) say there are projects they want to do but haven't started yet." "72% of homeowners who postponed or cancelled work cited budget or financial reasons." Method: about 1,200 homeowners, Mar–Apr 2026. https://intercom.help/angi/en/articles/15972206-the-backlog-boom-73-of-homeowners-have-projects-waiting
- **Line:** "A quote that went quiet usually isn't a no. 71% of homeowners put off at least one project last year, and interior painting was one of the most put off (Angi, 2025)."

## 7. Formula: turning the owner's own export into a loss figure

```
window     = quotes first sent in the last 24 months (owner can change it)
exclude    = drafts, $0, duplicates (same client + address within 7 days: keep the latest)
won        = has Job # OR approved/converted
said_no    = declined with a customer reason, OR owner marks "they said no"
open       = not won, last sent < 14 days ago          -> shown as "still fresh", not counted
quiet      = not won AND not said_no AND last sent >= 14 days ago
             (awaiting response; changes requested with no newer quote;
              expired / auto-declined / archived with no reason: a software close is not a customer no)
decided    = won + said_no + quiet

QUIET RATE        = quiet / decided                 (by count; the headline %)
QUIET RATE $      = Σ$quiet / Σ$decided
QUIET $           = Σ quote total of quiet          (also show the median; any single quote > 5× median
                                                     goes on "left for you to call" and out of the headline)
LEAD MONEY        = Σ CPL(source) over quiet quotes whose source is paid
                    fallback: quiet × paid_share (owner slider, no default) × CPL (owner-entered;
                    else the trade figure from §4, labelled with its source)
YOUR TIME         = quiet × hours per quote (owner slider, blank by default; never a made-up default)
IF ASKED (range)  = quiet × {1.5%, 2.7%, 4%} × median quiet quote
                    label: "What our first three shops saw. Owner-reported, no comparison group. Estimate."
Age split: 0–90 days / 3–12 months / 12–24 months. Past 180 days, notes never repeat the old price.
```

**Sample display, in owner voice:**
- "You sent 412 quotes. 163 never got a yes or a no. That's $486,400, or 40% of what you quoted, sitting quiet."
- "About a third of those came from Google. At about $38 a request (tree, Google LSA, 2026), that's around $2,100 you paid for people nobody finished with."
- "If yours go like our first three shops, about 4 book. Roughly $10,000. Your free 150 shows the real number."

Say "sitting quiet" or "never got an answer", **not "you lost $X"**. Some of those people hired someone else, and the owner can't tell which ones, because nobody asked.

## 9. What we must NOT say

- "You're losing X%" or "you'll add 11–15%". "The biggest shops say 11–15%" without "nearly half of $10M+ contractors".
- "PCA 2025: painters close 22% / 38% never decided / 28% color paralysis": no traceable source.
- "78% hire the first to respond" (Lead Connect), or its lookalike Moneypenny 2026 "83% of U.S. consumers choose the business that responds first". Moneypenny is an answering-service vendor, the figure is stated preference, and it is too close to the banned claim.
- Thumbtack "3x / 25% / 50% more likely hired if you respond in X minutes": no primary Thumbtack source found. A Thumbtack admin said "I can't remember the statistic". Also Yelp "4x more requests": unverified.
- "62% of calls go unanswered": 411 Locals, 2016, 85 businesses. Also "48% of home-service calls unanswered" (a misreading of Invoca's 52%).
- Blue Grid / Tree Traction "cost per booked job $542 Angi / $168 LSA", "lawn close rate 13%", "same-day quotes close 50–65%", "contractors send estimates only 10–25% of the time", "25–40% of estimates recoverable", "ServiceTitan 2024 Pulse HVAC 35–45%": all unsourced vendor or SEO content.
- Anything already in `BANNED_STATS`: 80%/5 follow-ups, 44/48/64/72/87%, HBR 7x/60x and InsideSales 21x/100x, 60–70% die from silence, and the rest.
- "Leads" on its own in copy: use "requests" or "people who asked for a price". The founder's own "No leads wasted." is fine as he wrote it.
