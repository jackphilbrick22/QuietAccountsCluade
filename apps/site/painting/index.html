Straight answers

What owners ask first.

What do I have to do?
One thing: forward your exports. Your software emails you your estimates and your past jobs, and you forward those emails to us. It takes about five minutes and we show you where to click. Then you read the first note with your name on it, change anything you want and text OK. The first notes go out the next weekday morning. You never give us a login and we never touch your email.
Won't this bug my customers?
Up to three short notes over a week or two, from your office, about the job you priced. Anyone who says stop is off the list that minute.
Who's actually writing to my customers?
Quiet Accounts, under your company's name. Every note goes out from our own sending address, never from your email or your phone, so your own email is never at risk. Nothing sends until you approve the first note, and a person, not a bot, reads every reply and texts it to you the same day. Text us and we'll delete your list.
What about estimates with no email?
It works on estimates and past customers with an email on them. Phone-only ones stay on your list for you to call.
I don't use Jobber.
Housecall Pro, or anything that exports your estimates and past jobs with a date, a price and an email, works too. Pick "Something else" in the form and we'll sort it out by text.
Why $250?
Nelson Fence, a fence company, booked 4 from its first 150 old quotes. You pay it only after the customer's booked.
When do I pay?
$250 per booked job. Never more than $1,000. Nothing books: $0. No card to start. When the first job books, we text you a link for the $250. It saves your card for the rest, and we text before every charge. What counts: someone wrote back to one of our notes and booked with you within 60 days. One charge per customer, however many jobs they book. Job cancels before the work? You get the $250 back. When it ends: when your list is done, about four weeks. No contract. Nothing renews.
What if I book someone on my own?
Only people who wrote back to our notes count. If we text you a booking that wasn't ours, reply NOT OURS before the charge date and it's off. Already charged? Tell Jack, and if it wasn't ours, he refunds it.
What if nothing comes back?
Then you owe nothing, and we'll be the ones to tell you. You'll know within three weeks of the first batch.
Is there a contract?
No. One pass through your list, then it's done. If your list keeps filling up, we'll offer to keep it going monthly.
How do I cancel?
Text 603-340-7673 and say you're done. That's the whole process. Nothing goes out after that. You pay only for jobs from our notes that booked before you cancelled.
