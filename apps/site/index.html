        Straight answers

        What owners ask first.

        What if nothing comes back?
        Then you don't pay. Any month nobody we followed up with asks you for a price, a visit or a date is free. Nothing to claim: we text you before every charge, and in a quiet month the text says it's free. The counting rules are written down before we start.
        What do I have to do?
        Send your quotes export, OK the first note, take the calls. Jobber, Housecall Pro, QuickBooks or a spreadsheet all work. No login, no new software.
        Will my customers feel pestered?
        Three short notes at most, about their own job, then we stop. “Stop” is off the list that minute. We never text or call your customers.
        Is a bot answering my customers?
        Software sorts every reply. A person checks anything that isn't a plain yes or a stop. Only routine answers go out on their own (“Thanks, Dave will call you today”), and they never promise a price or a date you didn't give.
        Does it send from my email?
        No. Notes come from an office inbox we set up in your company's name (“Sarah at Dave's Tree”). Your own email and domain are never touched.
        What if I already won it, or they said no on the phone?
        We check every name against your records first and skip anyone who booked. Text SKIP and their name, like SKIP Karen Whitfield, and they're off every list. Quotes over $10,000 and people with only a phone number go on your call list instead of getting a note, and a quote priced far above your usual for that work is held for you to look at first.
        What if I'm booked solid?
        Text BUSY until Nov 15. New work waits until about three weeks before you open up, and people who already said yes still get handled. Text OPEN when you want it back. PAUSE stops everything, and RESUME starts it again.
        What can I text back?
        About someone who's ready: BOOKED 2400, DONE or NO. When more than one person is waiting, add the #code from their text. Your first note waits for you: reply OK and it starts, or text what you'd change. SKIP and a name takes that person off every list. UNDO takes back a CANCEL within a day. HELP lists everything, and STOP turns off our texts to you (START turns them back on).
        How do I cancel?
        Text CANCEL. Billing stops that day, and so do the notes still waiting to go out. Didn't mean it? Text UNDO within a day.
