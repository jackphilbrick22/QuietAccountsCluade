        Straight answers

        What owners ask first.

        What exactly is the guarantee?
        Each month, if not one person we followed up with asks you for a price, a visit or a date, that month is free. You don't claim it. We text you before every charge, and in a quiet month the text says the month is free. It's counted on your ledger, and the counting rules are written down before we start. No contract, no renewal at a new price.
        What do I have to do?
        Forward one email with your quotes export, read the first note with your name on it, and take the calls. We set everything else up. You never give us a login and never learn new software.
        Will my customers feel spammed?
        Each person gets at most three short notes about their own job, from your office, then we stop. Anyone who says stop is off the list that minute. Nobody gets texted or called by us, ever.
        What if I already won the job, or they said no on the phone?
        We check every name against your records before a note goes out, and skip anyone who's already booked or came back. Quotes over $10,000 and people with only a phone number go on your call list instead, because those deserve your voice. Text us a name and they're off the list.
        Who reads the replies? Is it a bot?
        Every reply is read the minute it lands. Software sorts it, a person checks anything that isn't a plain yes or a stop, and you get a text. Only the routine answer goes out on its own ("Thanks, Dave will call you today"), and it never promises a price or a date.
        What if I'm booked solid?
        Text BUSY until Nov 15. New work waits until about three weeks before you open up, and people who already said yes still get handled. Text OPEN when you want it back.
        I don't use Jobber.
        Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, QuickBooks, or a spreadsheet. Anything that exports quotes with a date and an email works. Pick "Something else" below and we'll sort it out by text.
        How do I cancel?
        Text CANCEL. We text back what's still scheduled, you reply CANCEL YES, and that's it.
