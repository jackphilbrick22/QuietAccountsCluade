Straight answers

What owners ask first.

What do I have to do?
One thing. Your software emails you a report of past jobs; you forward that email to us. It takes about four minutes and we show you where to click. Then you read the first note with your name on it, change anything you want, and pick the day the first 25 go out. You never give us a login and we never touch your email.
Won't this annoy my customers?
Three short notes over about a week, from your office, about this season's work. They read like a note from the company that did the work last time, because that's what they are. Anyone who replies "stop" is removed that minute. The first 150 are free, so you see your own numbers before you pay anything.
Who sends the notes, and from where?
Quiet Accounts, under your company's name. The notes go out from a sending address we run, never from your email or your phone, so your own domain and deliverability are never at risk. Nothing sends until you approve the first note, and a person, not a bot, reads every reply and texts it to you the same day.
I don't use Jobber.
Service Autopilot, Yardbook, LMN, Aspire, Housecall Pro, QuickBooks. Anything that exports your customers or visits with a date and an email column works. Pick "Something else" below and we'll sort it out by text.
What if nothing comes back?
Then you owe nothing, and we'll be the ones to tell you. You'll know within three weeks of the first batch.
Do I have to get on a call?
No. The whole thing runs by text and email. If you'd rather hear a voice first, there's a 15-minute link at the bottom, but nobody needs it.
How do I cancel?
Text 603-340-7673 and say you're done. That's the whole process. Anything already scheduled that week still goes out; nothing after.
