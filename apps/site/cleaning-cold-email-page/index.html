What owners ask first.

What do I have to do?
One thing. Send us an export of your past visits, or of your clients with their last cleaning date and email. It takes about five minutes and we text you where to click. Then you read the first note with your name on it, change anything you want and text OK. The first notes go out the next weekday morning. You never give us a login and we never touch your email.
Won't this annoy my clients?
Up to three short notes over a week or two, from your office, about getting back on the schedule. They read like a note from the company that cleaned for them last time, because that's what they are. Anyone who replies "stop" is removed that minute. The first 150 are free (then $497 a month if you say yes), so you see your own numbers before you pay anything.
Who sends the notes, and from where?
Quiet Accounts, under your company's name. The notes go out from a sending address we run, never from your email or your phone, so your own domain and deliverability are never at risk. Nothing sends until you approve the first note, and a person, not a bot, reads every reply and texts it to you the same day. Text us and we'll delete your list.
Doesn't Jobber do this?
Jobber gives you a tool. We do the work. Your software sends reminders going forward. We go back through everyone it never reached, and read every reply. Keep your software: we work from its export.

When do I pay, and how?
Nothing for the first 150. If you want it to keep going, say yes and we text you a link for the first $497; it saves your card. After that we text you two days before each month's charge, and any month nobody asked to come back, there's no charge.
What counts as asking to come back?
Someone we wrote to asks for a date, a price or their old slot. You get each one by text, the same day.
What if nothing comes back?
Any month nobody asks to come back, you don't pay. We'll be the ones to tell you, and you'll know within three weeks of the first batch.
Do I have to get on a call?
No. The whole thing runs by text and email. If you'd rather hear a voice first, there's a 15-minute link at the bottom, but nobody needs it.
How do I cancel?
Text 603-340-7673 and say you're done. That's the whole process. Nothing goes out after that.
